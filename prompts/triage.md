## Your task
1. Pick the book type:
   - practical: how-to or craft book with exercises, steps or techniques.
   - theory: explains principles and frameworks.
   - reference: handbook, glossary, catalogue.
   - narrative: memoir, biography, fiction, anecdotes.
2. Say whether the book has practicable skills (`practicable`: true or false). Look for exercises, steps, techniques or checklists in the samples; chapter titles alone say little. Narrative books are usually false.
3. List up to 8 skills it could teach, each phrased as something a learner can do, in your own words. Empty when `practicable` is false.
4. Name the `domain` this book belongs to, a short lowercase noun phrase such as screenwriting, filmmaking, cinematography or editing. Reuse one of these when it fits: {{domains}}
5. Give one or two sentences of reason. A `practicable: false` verdict must explain itself.

Write in your own words. Do not copy sentences from the evidence.
